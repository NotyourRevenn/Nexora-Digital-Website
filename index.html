Got Questions?

Questions, Answered.

Everything you need to know about partnering with Nexora Digital for sustainable brand growth.

Which plan should I pick for my business?

Starter suits early-stage brands and creators building an active social media presence and initial audience. Growth (our most popular tier) is designed for brands ready to actively generate sales leads and customer acquisition via paid Meta & Google Ads. Premium is built for brands wanting full-scale omnichannel domination with 30+ posts, influencer collaboration, and a dedicated senior account manager.

Can you build something custom for our specific industry?

Absolutely. Tell us your goals, target audience, and current numbers during our discovery call, and we will tailor an exact custom package combining the right channel mix for your industry.

Do you offer websites, landing pages, and branding?

Yes! Full-stack website development (WordPress, Next.js, Webflow, Shopify), high-converting UI/UX landing pages, and logo/brand identity design are all available as add-ons to any growth package or standalone projects.

How will I know the campaigns are delivering ROI?

Every plan includes regular performance reporting. We track bottom-line metrics: Cost Per Acquisition (CPA), Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), Qualified Leads, and Revenue Generated — not just superficial vanity likes.

How quickly can we launch after onboarding?

Onboarding typically takes 3 to 5 business days. During this window, we audit your accounts, install tracking pixels, map out your creative strategy, and prepare initial ad creatives for your approval.
